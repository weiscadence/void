WHAT YOU'RE WATCHING

Energy enters at the bottom and thins as it rises, so the pyramid caps its own height — that's why the tower is short, not broken. A middle niche isn't just connected to the ones above it; it feeds them. Cut it, and its dependents starve. In self-feeding mode every niche makes its own food, so cutting a "parent" only snips a line — nobody upstairs even notices. Same cut, opposite outcome: that's how you know the collapse is about energy, not wiring.

HONESTY BOX
  • Measured, headless, 3 seeds: knock out a mid niche → 100% of the descendants collapse in the energetic world vs 0% in the self-feeding control. Real, controlled energetic dependency. (In-browser above is a smaller live run of the same engine.)
  • Re-invention EXISTS, but only in a thin window (updated). Hard knockout kills the lineage, so nothing regrows (0/3). The gentle knockout leaves the branch alive but starving, and then a grace sweep found the tradeoff: at grace 0 the branch dies before rescue; at grace 0.20–0.25 it crashes ~50% then a NEW, differently-keyed niche spontaneously re-adopts it and heals it back to ~92% (2–3 of 6 seeds, 3–4 re-adoption events); above 0.30 it just self-feeds and the knockout stops meaning anything. Enough hardiness to survive the crash, not so much the crash never happens. Move the slider and watch the re-adoptions counter.
  • Still not a breakthrough — but a real, measured positive. Spontaneous re-connection happens, and we have the window for it. Honest limits: it's noisy (2–3/6, not reliable), and the "grace" is a dial I added, so the system doesn't yet self-tune into the window on its own. Next: make the branch's hardiness emergent, not a knob.
