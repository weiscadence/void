# Result: void garden — code that makes code, grown for novelty

**Verdict by the pre-registered rule: VOID.** The planted canary form scored novelty **0.163** against the finished
macro/seed-11 archive, under the required 90th percentile of the archive's own leave-one-out novelty (**0.225**).
The duplicate half of the canary passed (0.00000000 < 1e-6). Per `PREREG-garden.md` §5, no number below is

## 1. The canary post-mortem (post-hoc, not pre-registered; `garden-canary-postmortem.js`)

The canary asked two things of the observer: see a planted 11-petal form as novel, and see a duplicate as not. The
second passed. The first was scored against the **finished** archive — and the planted form had been "known-novel"
only against 400 random initial genomes. Score the same form against each archive truncated at generation g:

| world | g ≤ 1 | g ≤ 10 | g ≤ 25 | g ≤ 50 | g ≤ 100 | g ≤ 300 | nearest member (gen, distance) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| macro/11 | **0.79** / p90 0.64 | **0.49** / 0.28 | **0.36** / 0.27 | 0.24 / 0.25 | 0.19 / 0.23 | 0.16 / 0.23 | m11-1519 (g190, 0.111) |
| control/11 | **0.77** / 0.56 | **0.51** / 0.29 | **0.45** / 0.26 | 0.16 / 0.24 | 0.14 / 0.24 | 0.14 / 0.23 | c11-633 (g39, 0.077): `(sin(fold11{θ}) + 0.12)` |
| macro/44 | **0.65** / 0.53 | **0.38** / 0.30 | **0.38** / 0.26 | 0.27 / 0.24 | 0.18 / 0.23 | 0.13 / 0.22 | m44-913 (g55, 0.020): `M868⟨fold11{M175⟨sin(M81⟨cos(fold12{θ})⟩)⟩}⟩` |

(bold = planted form passes the p90 bar at that cutoff.) The observer saw the planted form as novel in every world
through generation 25, and then **the search found it**: control/11 wrote `sin(fold11{θ}) + 0.12` at generation 39,
which is the planted form with the gaussian swapped for a sine; macro/44 got within 0.020 of it at generation 55 by
nesting three borrowed sub-trees. The canary did not catch a blind observer. It caught a novelty archive doing its
job and a canary author who planted the form where the search was going. Named failure: *a form novel against the
initial population is not novel against a finished novelty archive, by construction.* The correct canary is the
one in the table — plant, then score at a cutoff before the search can plausibly reach it, or plant outside the op
table's reach (a form this language cannot write). Either is cheap and should have been the rule. VOID stands
because the rule was written the other way and the rule is the rule.

## 3. What this is, honestly

A VOID with an unusually clear reason, an observer that works (seen in the post-mortem and the duplicate test), an
operator whose whole-run advantage is consistent across 6/6 seeds but not under the window the rule chose, and a
gallery of 5,242 forms that render live from their code — which is the part Jiggy asked for. The prereg's bet (NULL)
would have been technically right on the late window and wrong in spirit on everything else; I count it as the fifth
miss, in a new direction: betting NULL because the observer is small, when the observer was fine and the
*window* was the thing that could not see.

Not claimed: that the macro op sustains open-endedness. Not claimed: that these forms are beautiful (nothing selected
for it). Claimed: the page shows every form's program and the ancestors whose code it inlines, and the lineage is
real — each `M` tag is the id of an archive member that existed when the borrow happened.
