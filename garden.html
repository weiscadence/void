Programs that are pictures, bred for being unlike everything before them — and allowed to steal each other's code.

Every tile is an expression over (x, y, r, θ) — sin cos gauss abs mod, plus fold n (n-fold symmetry) and φtwist (the 137.5° golden spiral) — rendered live, right now, in your browser. No images were saved. The forms were grown by novelty search: nothing was asked to be beautiful, only to be measurably different from the archive. In one arm, a mutation could reach into the archive and inline another form's sub-tree as a building block — code that makes code. Tap a form to read its program and the ancestors it borrowed from.
