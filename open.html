VOID 〰️ · OPEN VOCABULARY

The night void stopped settling.

Every world here before this one plateaued — invented a while, then stopped getting more complex. The reason was always the same: a fixed menu of things an organism could be. You can't keep getting richer out of a closed dictionary; you can only stack more copies of the words you already have.

So the genome stopped being a parts-list and became a tiny program that computes a key — and the set of possible keys isn't a menu, it's generated, open. A cell feeds you only if your key fits its lock, and with niche construction you can plant your own key as a lock. Three worlds below, identical but for one thing — how open the vocabulary is. THE RESULT — TWO LEVERS THAT EACH PLATEAU, STACKED, DON'T (MEASURED, 40k TICKS)

Headless, pre-registered, 4 seeds. Fixed menu freezes at ~7 stable niches — the closed dictionary, exactly as predicted. Open vocabulary alone jumps ~6× to ~39 and then re-plateaus: an open map is the fuel, but with nothing driving exploration it settles into the niches drift can reach. Open vocabulary + niche construction is the only arm that keeps going — 46 → 90+ stable niches over 40,000 ticks, still rising where the others froze by 2,000, program complexity climbing alongside it.

The mechanism is a clean three-way split: fixed menu is an engine with no fuel; open-without-building is fuel with no engine; only together — construction keeps planting locks in new regions of an open space, so new niches beget new builders — do you get a climb that doesn't run out. It ties the whole project together: niche construction settled into a stable equilibrium on a fixed vocabulary; on an open one, the same mechanism doesn't settle.

HONESTY BOX

What's true: three-way dissociation, pre-registered, replicated, and the niches are stable (held for hundreds of ticks), not novelty churn — so this is not silica's endless-drift trap. It's the first result here where the defining plateau is broken, not just relocated.

What's NOT claimed: that this is open-ended evolution solved. Open+construction beats open-alone in 9 of 10 seeds — a robust direction, but with big variance (+41 to −10 niches; on some worlds construction even hurts), so any single run you watch may not show it. The climb also decelerates — not a proven runaway.

And we found the next ceiling: once the menu is gone, the binding limit is space, not vocabulary — raising the key-space cap 16× changes nothing, but a bigger grid keeps the climb going. So the niche count is partly just filling the box. Ceilings all the way down; we lifted one and named the next. A real brick, honestly sized — not the whole wall.
