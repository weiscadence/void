Can a creature invent a useful part once, and build on it — instead of reinventing it every time? That's the question. Below is the live experiment.

status — withdrawn, and being re-run properly

What you see below runs the old version, where keeping a part is free. Mostly-empty strips is the honest answer to that version: if a part costs nothing and does nothing, evolution drops it.

The first conclusion we drew from that — "evolution keeps losing its parts" — was wrong. Our generation counter was broken: 1,200 "generations" was really about 16 generations of actual descent. Fixed, and the libraries fill right up. Nothing was being lost. There were barely any births.

The real mistake: this gives permission to reuse. It never makes not reusing expensive. Those are different things, and only the second one is a selection pressure.
