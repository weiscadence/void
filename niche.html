What if a thing could remodel the world that judges it?

A spicy pepper isn't adapting to its environment — it's editing it, growing a fruit only birds will eat so birds become the ones who spread its seed. That's niche construction: the organism reaches out and changes the rules of the world it lives in. Both grids below start from the same hostile world — most of the ground suits only one kind (the dim majority). The only difference is the switch on the right: can organisms terraform their own cell to a resource they can eat?

Colour is which resource a cell holds; a dot is an organism sitting on it. Count the colours that persist — that's how many kinds coexist. Left, the hostile world squeezes everyone toward one. Right, constructors manufacture the niches the world refused them.

THE FINDING — CONSTRUCTION SUSTAINS COEXISTENCE (MEASURED, 8/8)

Run headless before this page existed, paired seeds (same world, only the switch differs), with the effect size and threshold fixed before the run: an established constructor community holds +0.84 more coexisting kinds than the control — positive in all 8 seeds, effect nearly 3× its own noise. With a heritable variant (parents prepare the ground their offspring land on) it rises to +1.24. The mechanism is exactly the theory: more ways to make a living means more kinds can coexist (competitive exclusion — the number of coexisting types is bounded by the number of limiting factors, and construction manufactures limiting factors).

The effect is stable, not a fluke of when you look: held flat at +1.17 from tick 1,000 all the way to 20,000. And it's causal, not just correlated — stop the building mid-run and let the built ground decay, and coexistence collapses straight back to the control baseline in a few thousand ticks. The constructors are ecosystem engineers, and their engineering is load-bearing.

Two catches the same runs found: construction can't bootstrap — drop the trait into a world that already has one working niche and it's selected out as dead weight before the specialists who'd benefit can establish. And it isn't inherited: the coexistence is actively maintained, rebuilt every generation, not passed down as a made world.

HONESTY BOX

What's true: the coexistence gain is real, pre-registered, replicated 8/8 seeds, stable to 20,000 ticks, and knocked out cleanly (remove construction → it collapses). The world is genuinely hostile (control holds ~1.5 kinds) so there's real room, not a ceiling artifact — an earlier version false-passed on +0.008 because I wrote a threshold with no minimum effect size, and that false pass is why this one has one.

What's ruled OUT: that descendants inherit the world their ancestors built. I built the instrument for it and it reads ~zero even among builders — this is the active pepper (a maintained niche), not the heritable one. And it's a stable equilibrium, which is the honest opposite of open-endedness: it settles. Nobody has solved open-ended evolution in a web page, us included — this is one clean measured brick, not the wall.
