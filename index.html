the one line §2
An artificial ecology plateaus for a reason that is a theorem, not a tuning problem. Competitive exclusion: the number of coexisting types is bounded by the number of limiting factors. One resource, one winner. Every substrate here — GP trees, reaction-diffusion, Flow Lenia, self-replicating programs, a graph of niches — hit that wall or one of its cousins. The notebook is the record of lifting each ceiling and naming the next.

Under the continuous law, does the climb ever stop?

Finding 10 removed the integer fixed point and the frozen worlds became climbers that were still rising at 30,000 ticks. That left the notebook's oldest claim — "no world is unbounded", climbers capped at 74–105 — untested, because it was measured under the integer law. So: fresh seeds, 100,000 ticks, CROWD 6.1 (12 seeds) and real-valued ±5% noise (4 seeds, a thin replication after the noise arm measured 2.2× the CPU and the design was cut mid-flight, in writing). A world has plateaued at cutoff W if its stable-niche count sets no new max (±2) in the last W ticks; W = 20k / 30k / 40k. The pooled climb curve is fit with linear, logarithmic and saturating models and the residuals are shown, not just the winner.

Pre-stated: CEILING if ≥ 75% of seeds plateau at 20k and the curve saturates; NO CEILING SEEN if ≤ 25% plateau and the curve is linear or log; MIXED otherwise, sub-case named. Canary: the original law must re-halt seeds 207 and 307 at crowd 18. Decoy: CROWD 5 must halt at 15. My bet, written before the run: ceiling, saturating at 110–140 — the world runs out of cells, not ideas.

break the fixed point · pre-registered · sha256 in repo

The frozen basin was integer arithmetic

Finding 09 ended on a number: every frozen world sat with its exposed niches at crowd exactly 18, where harvest 4 / (1 + crowd/6) equals upkeep 1 and nothing can ever be born or die. If that is the cause of the freeze and not a symptom, moving the constant to 6.1 — balance at 18.3, no integer solution — must make the halt impossible. Real-valued ±5% noise on each harvest should do the same. A decoy moves the constant ten times further, to 5, but keeps an integer balance (15): it should still halt, at 15. And the original law must reproduce yesterday's halts to the tick, or none of this is evidence.

Pre-stated: one halted known-frozen world under 6.1 kills the mechanism. If the former-frozen seeds then reach ≥65 stable niches they are climbers (the fixed point was the cage); if ≤1/5 do and the 20k→30k drift is under +5 they stall in a slow basin. My bet, written before the run: stall.
