the one line §2
An artificial ecology plateaus for a reason that is a theorem, not a tuning problem. Competitive exclusion: the number of coexisting types is bounded by the number of limiting factors. One resource, one winner. Every substrate here — GP trees, reaction-diffusion, Flow Lenia, self-replicating programs, a graph of niches — hit that wall or one of its cousins. The notebook is the record of lifting each ceiling and naming the next.

what sets the level? · one dial at a time · pre-registered · sha256 in repo

Is the ~100-niche level an energy budget, a crowd arithmetic, or a turnover balance?

Finding 12 showed the level ignores room. Ecology offers three controllers of niche number: energy (the more-individuals hypothesis — richness tracks how many individuals the energy supply holds), balance arithmetic (a full key holds CROWD·(YIELD−1) organisms, so if energy fills keys rather than making them, population moves and niches do not), and turnover (Hubbell's θ = 2Jν — richness is community size × origination rate, so mutation load moves niches without moving population). Test: the 1× grid, CROWD 6.1, 40k ticks, 4 seeds, one dial at a time — yield 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 (balance crowds 12.2 / 18.3 / 24.4 / 30.5, none integer) and mutation load ×0.25 / ×0.5 / ×1 / ×2; yield 4 / ×1 is the stored finding-11 baseline. The STABLE_AGE threshold is a lens, not a world dial, so it is reported as a curve (held ≥ 100 … ≥ 3,200 ticks) at no cost.

Pre-stated: ENERGY-LIMITED if the niche elasticity on (yield−1) is ≥ 0.5, at least half the population's, and monotone; ARITHMETIC-INVARIANT if |niche elasticity| ≤ 0.2 while population's ≥ 0.5; INVERSE if ≤ −0.3; MIXED otherwise. TURNOVER-LIMITED if a mutation level moves niches ≥ 15% with population within 10%; MUTATION-INERT if all levels move niches < 10%. Canary: the original law must re-halt 207 and 307 at 18. Decoy: influx reads like an energy dial but the engine only uses it in conserved mode, so influx = 100 must reproduce the stored baseline sample for sample (this doubles as the harness check). My bet, written before the run: ENERGY-LIMITED, and double mutation lowers niches. The last three bets missed.

Of Hubbell's θ = 2Jν this world keeps ν and drops J. The bet was wrong on both halves — the fourth miss in a row.

geometric or ecological? · pre-registered · sha256 in repo

Is the climb slowing because the world is running out of room?

Finding 11 left a logarithm bending toward the 50×50 grid's arithmetic cap and said the bend was probably the room. Cheapest decisive test: the same seeds and the same law (CROWD 6.1) on grids of 1× / 1.49× / 2.02× / 3.03× the area (sides 50, 61, 71, 87; founders scaled with area), to a common 40,000 ticks. If room is the limit, stable niches scale with area (exponent α near 1) and the fraction-of-cap curves collapse onto one line. If the law is the limit, the count stays put whatever the room.

Pre-stated: GEOMETRIC if α ≥ 0.75 and ≥ 6/8 seeds hold ≥ 1.5× as many niches on the 2× grid as on their own 50×50 twin; ECOLOGICAL if α ≤ 0.25 and ≤ 2/8 twins gain ≥ 25%; MIXED otherwise, sub-case named. Canary: the original law must re-halt 207 and 307 at 18. Harness: the 50×50 re-run must reproduce the stored finding-11 baseline sample for sample, or it cannot be reused. My bet, written before the run: GEOMETRIC, α 0.8–1.0; reach pinned.

Under the continuous law, does the climb ever stop?

Finding 10 removed the integer fixed point and the frozen worlds became climbers that were still rising at 30,000 ticks. That left the notebook's oldest claim — "no world is unbounded", climbers capped at 74–105 — untested, because it was measured under the integer law. So: fresh seeds, 100,000 ticks, CROWD 6.1 (12 seeds) and real-valued ±5% noise (4 seeds, a thin replication after the noise arm measured 2.2× the CPU and the design was cut mid-flight, in writing). A world has plateaued at cutoff W if its stable-niche count sets no new max (±2) in the last W ticks; W = 20k / 30k / 40k. The pooled climb curve is fit with linear, logarithmic and saturating models and the residuals are shown, not just the winner.

The frozen basin was integer arithmetic

Finding 09 ended on a number: every frozen world sat with its exposed niches at crowd exactly 18, where harvest 4 / (1 + crowd/6) equals upkeep 1 and nothing can ever be born or die. If that is the cause of the freeze and not a symptom, moving the constant to 6.1 — balance at 18.3, no integer solution — must make the halt impossible. Real-valued ±5% noise on each harvest should do the same. A decoy moves the constant ten times further, to 5, but keeps an integer balance (15): it should still halt, at 15. And the original law must reproduce yesterday's halts to the tick, or none of this is evidence.

Pre-stated: one halted known-frozen world under 6.1 kills the mechanism. If the former-frozen seeds then reach ≥65 stable niches they are climbers (the fixed point was the cage); if ≤1/5 do and the 20k→30k drift is under +5 they stall in a slow basin. My bet, written before the run: stall.
